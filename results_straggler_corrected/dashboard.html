Where it works: one expensive task near the tail of a queue. The algorithm estimates batch cost from known task metadata, forms variable-size batches and submits the longest batches first in one pool dispatch. On this targeted workload it was faster than fixed chunk 4 in all ten fresh paired runs. Limit: it did not win every workload in the broader five-class test.
