Where it works: one expensive task near the tail of a queue. The algorithm estimates batch cost from known task metadata, forms variable-size batches and submits the longest batches first in one pool dispatch. On this targeted workload it was faster than fixed chunk 4 in all ten fresh paired runs. Limit: it did not win every workload in the broader five-class test.

Fresh tail-straggler test

Ten seeds (8001–8010); one 40× C task randomly among the last eight of 192 tasks; two workers. End-to-end wall time includes process startup and teardown. The test plan was recorded before measurement. Dataset, order and checksums are saved. Validation: PASS.

Declared method: balanced_32. Median paired ratio to fixed_4 = 0.851; lower than 1 is faster. The paired ratio is the primary result, not an isolated best run.
